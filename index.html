Air guitar

Based on Music Maker by Hashem Al-Ghaili.

Stretch strings in the air and play them with your fingertips.

  1. 1Stretch. Pinch thumb to index finger on both hands, pull your hands apart, then let go.
  2. 2Shape. Pinch an end to move it. Shorter strings sound higher. Drop an end on the other end to remove the string. Pinch the middle to pick its instrument.
  3. 3Play. Sweep a fingertip across a string. Switch to Play mode so nothing moves by accident.

Up to eight hands can play at once. Sit 40 to 80 cm from the camera with light on your hands. The hand tracker loads from the internet the first time.
